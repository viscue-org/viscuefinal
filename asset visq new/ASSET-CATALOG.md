# Viscue Secondary Visual Asset Collection

Twenty-four isolated, square PNG cut-outs for login, onboarding, empty-state, and product-marketing surfaces.

## Shared art direction

- Premium retro-futurist editorial product photography
- One cohesive subject per asset with generous transparent padding
- Viscue steel blue (`#5B7593`), optical glass, satin chrome, and warm paper-white
- Soft neutral studio light and restrained grounding shadows
- No text, logos, people, interfaces, watermarks, collage, or background scenes

## Composition guidance

Use one primary asset per viewport. Place it near an outer edge or behind a content panel at low visual dominance. Avoid combining more than two assets on the same screen; the collection is designed to provide variety between pages, not to recreate the dense reference collage.
